2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that may be stored on a visitor’s device when a website is accessed. They can help websites remember preferences, support functionality, understand website usage, or improve visitor experience.

Similar technologies, such as local storage, pixels, tags, or browser-based identifiers, may also be used for related purposes.

3. Necessary cookies

Necessary cookies or local storage items help the website function correctly. These may support basic features such as page behavior, cookie consent storage, security, accessibility, and technical reliability.

For example, this website may store a visitor’s cookie preference choice so the cookie banner does not appear again on every page after the visitor accepts or declines.

Necessary cookies are generally required for the website to work properly and may not be disabled through the website’s cookie banner.

4. Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies may be used to understand how visitors interact with the website, which pages are viewed, how users move through the site, and where improvements may be needed.

Analytics information is generally used to improve website structure, content clarity, performance, usability, and marketing communication.

Where analytics cookies are used, visitors may be able to accept or decline them through the cookie banner or manage them through browser settings.

5. Marketing cookies

Marketing cookies may be used to understand campaign performance, measure visitor interactions, or support more relevant marketing communication.

This website does not require visitors to accept marketing cookies in order to browse the site or contact TPN s. r. o.

If marketing cookies are used, they may depend on third-party platforms or measurement tools, and visitors may manage their preferences through the cookie banner or browser controls where available.

6. Preference cookies and local storage

Preference cookies or local storage may remember visitor choices, such as cookie consent selection or interface-related preferences.

On this website, cookie preference choices may be saved in the browser using local storage under a consent-related key. This helps keep the banner from resetting after a visitor has already made a selection.

7. Third-party cookies and external services

This website may use or link to third-party services that can set or rely on cookies, scripts, or similar technologies. These may include services for maps, fonts, icons, animation libraries, analytics, or marketing measurement.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy practices. TPN s. r. o. does not control how third-party providers manage cookies or similar technologies.

Visitors should review the cookie and privacy policies of third-party services they choose to access.

8. Cookie consent banner

The website includes a cookie consent banner with options to accept or decline non-essential cookies.

The banner does not close automatically. It remains visible until the visitor chooses an option. Once a choice is made, the selection may be saved in the visitor’s browser so the banner does not reappear on every page.

Declining non-essential cookies should not prevent visitors from navigating the website or using basic contact options.

9. How users can manage cookies

Visitors can manage cookies in several ways:

  • Use the cookie banner to accept or decline non-essential cookies where available.
  • Adjust browser settings to block, delete, or limit cookies.
  • Clear local storage or site data through browser settings.
  • Use privacy controls offered by third-party platforms where relevant.

Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality, preferences, analytics accuracy, or visitor experience.

10. Clearing cookie consent

If a visitor wants to reset the cookie banner choice, they can clear browser site data, cookies, or local storage for this website through their browser settings.

After clearing the saved preference, the cookie banner may appear again on the next website visit.
